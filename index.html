Mimic Studio needs your camera

This site watches your hands and body to learn gestures.

  • Nothing is uploaded. Every frame is processed inside this browser tab.
  • Nothing is recorded to disk unless you press Record yourself.
  • No account, no server. Close the tab and it is all gone.

What is happening?

Two AI models are running on every single frame:

Hand Landmarker finds 21 points on each hand — 4 on each finger plus one on the wrist — for up to two hands at once.

Pose Landmarker finds 33 points on your whole body: shoulders, elbows, hips, knees and so on.

Each point comes back as an x, y and z number. The lines you see are just those dots joined up in the right order, like a dot-to-dot puzzle.
